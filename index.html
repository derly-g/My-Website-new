Derly Gutierrez

I’m a graphic designer with experience in traditional art, video editing, and design communication. I enjoy working on personal projects in my free time, especially video editing, branding, and poster design. I’m really interested in film, music videos, and poster design, and I’d like to explore photography and magazine design more in the future. A lot of my interests came from finding media that really stood out to me. I’m a big fan of movies and go to the theater every couple of weeks. I also have a small DVD collection. One thing I really like about film is how closely it connects to video editing and graphic design. I especially enjoy movies with strong opening title sequences. Music videos are another big influence for me. Because they’re short, editors have to be creative and find ways to grab attention quickly, which I find really inspiring. Photography and magazine design are still areas I’m learning about. I love looking at strong photography, especially in magazines. I find it interesting how magazines combine images and text in a way that feels engaging and easy to read.

Project 1

This project is a hand made cardboard art redesign of Agnes Pelton’s "The Primal Wing"(1933) with a mixed use of paint, marker, paper and cardboard. Originally created for a Design assignment, it showcases a fun-crafty sense of design and illustration.

Project 2

This project is a Helvetica typeface edited poster and infographic. Originally created for a Typography assignment, it showcases the Helvetica font with a brief summary on the typeface, who created it, and how it's used.

Project 3

This project is a edited movie poster of the movie "Bones And All" with a mix of photo editing and illustration. Originally created for a Digital Imaging assignment, it showcases editing and illustration experience.
